How a trade is decided

Six steps, in order. A pool must pass every step to be bought, and each step records why it rejected a pool.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Collect candidate tokens from several sources, tagged so source bias can be measured.

    —
  2. 2

    Filter

    Age, liquidity, volume, momentum band and buy pressure. Missing data counts as not evaluable, never as a pass.

    —
  3. 3

    Safety checks

    Mint and freeze authority, holder concentration, Token-2022 traps, liquidity lock, a simulated sell.

    —
  4. 4

    Paper entry

    Fill at the next candle after the signal, with fees, slippage, latency and account rent charged.

    —
  5. 5

    Manage the exit

    Stop loss, take profit, trailing stop, time limit, liquidity drop. Ambiguous candles resolve to the worse outcome.

    —
  6. 6

    Verdict

    Out-of-sample results only, compared with random entries. The go / no-go is decided by coded thresholds.

    —

    What this dashboard does not show yet
    • It is a snapshot exported by the bot and refreshed when Claude republishes it; it does not stream from the work session.
    • Paper trades, the per-filter funnel, backtest results and the cost sensitivity table appear once those tasks produce data.
    • Candidates are pools listed today, so tokens that rugged and vanished are under-represented. Backtest numbers carry that survivorship caveat.
    • Liquidity on the charts is today's value, not historical, because the price-history source does not provide it.
    • Live trading is off in code and configuration. Nothing on this page can switch it on.
